Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sphynx processes information when you use the Sphynx Discord bot or website.

1. Information We Process

Depending on enabled features, Sphynx may process:

  • Discord user IDs, server IDs and channel IDs.
  • Command names and configuration settings.
  • Messages where required for a specific feature.
  • Moderation records and ticket information.
  • Technical information needed to operate the Service.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide bot features, manage configurations, operate moderation and support tools, prevent abuse and troubleshoot errors.

3. Storage and Retention

Information may be stored by the bot and its hosting or database providers. Retention depends on the feature and configuration. Deleting a Discord message does not necessarily delete a separate bot record.

4. Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers or disclosed when legally required.

5. Your Choices

You can stop using Sphynx at any time. Server administrators can remove the bot from their servers. For data requests, contact the Sphynx team through its official support channel.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Sphynx is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Users must follow Discord's age requirements.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the Service changes. Updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Sphynx team through its official support server.
